For Contractors

Want customer leads from Fresh View? Apply to join the contractor network. Contractors must provide accurate business information and are responsible for their own licenses, insurance, work, pricing, scheduling, warranties, corrections, and customer claims.

Referred jobs are subject to a 7% referral/marketing fee based on the total amount the client pays for the referred job.

Important Legal Notice

Fresh View Property Services is a referral and scheduling coordination platform. Fresh View Property Services does not personally perform contractor work and does not represent that it holds a home improvement contractor license.

Customers contract directly with the independent service provider performing the work. Independent service providers are responsible for their own estimates, contracts, pricing, insurance, licenses where required, jobsite conduct, workmanship, warranties, corrections, damages, delays, and service-related claims.

Fresh View Property Services does not supervise contractor work, guarantee workmanship, or assume responsibility for the independent provider's performance.
